# iOS Shortcut Setup

## Add to Home Screen (PWA)
1. Open Safari on iOS
2. Go to https://your-vercel-domain.vercel.app
3. Tap Share icon (square with arrow)
4. Tap "Add to Home Screen"
5. Name it "CodePhantom"
6. Tap "Add"

## Create Siri Shortcut
1. Open Shortcuts app
2. Tap "+" to create new shortcut
3. Add action: "Open URL"
4. Enter: https://t.me/YourBotUsername
5. Tap "Next"
6. Name it "Open ASTRO"
7. Tap "Add to Siri"
8. Say "Hey Siri, open ASTRO" to launch

## Enable Voice Input
1. In Telegram, go to your bot chat
2. Tap the microphone icon
3. Speak your message
4. Tap send
